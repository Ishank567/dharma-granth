Unique structure: a sustained dialogue between rescuer and rescued. Core myth: Hiranyaksha (extractive principle) seizes Earth and submerges her; Vishnu dives as cosmic Boar and lifts her back. Ch1 Varaha Avatara: 'vārāhaṃ rūpamāsthāya...rasātalaṃ gatvā' — the divine takes the form most suited to reach the suffering, descends to the deepest point, and restores dignity (not just removes harm); Hiranyaksha = extractive principle vs. Bhudevi = sustaining principle (planetary boundaries).
