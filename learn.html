कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन

karmaṇyevādhikāraste mā phaleṣu kadācana

What is the teaching of this verse?

तुम्हारा अधिकार केवल कर्म करने में है, फल में कभी नहीं।

You have a right only to perform your duty; the fruits thereof are not your concern.

Focus on the action, not the outcome. This leads to better performance and inner peace.
