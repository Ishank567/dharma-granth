Kaushitaki Upanishad

कौषीतकि उपनिषद्

A principal Upanishad of the Rigveda. Famous for Indra's teaching to Pratardana: prana (conscious breath) is the only true Self. Introduces the two post-death paths: devayana (non-return) and pitriyana (rebirth).
